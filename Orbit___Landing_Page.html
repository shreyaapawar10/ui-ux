✦ Five spaces, one orbit

Everything stillness needs, nothing it doesn't

Each tab answers one question: what does this moment need from me?

Home

Four quick starts — Meditate, Focus, Sleep, Journal — and today's plan with a progress bar. No streak counter on this screen.

Goals

Set 1-year, 5-year and 10-year goals across career, health, finance, relationships and personal growth. Each ring shows milestones reached.

Meditate

The sessions you'll return to, filtered by Focus, Sleep, Stress or Energy. A featured pick each day, from 5-minute resets to 30-minute wind-downs.

Explore

For finding something new: recommended methods such as breathwork and yoga nidra, and a browsable catalogue by length and level.

Profile

Your minutes, streak and weekly practice, plus a few milestone badges. Kept for you, never ranked against anyone.

✦ Good questions

Before you start

Is Orbit religious or led by a teacher?

No. Orbit is secular by default: no single teacher and no dogma. Some practices, like yoga nidra, come from older traditions. Orbit describes them plainly and doesn't ask you to believe anything.

What does the AI planner do?

You type or say what you need today. Orbit turns it into a task list for your plan, and you can accept it, edit it or ask for a new one. It's a planner, not a teacher or a person.

How long are the sessions?

From 5-minute resets like Quick Reset to 30-minute sleep sessions. On the Meditate tab, filter by Focus, Sleep, Stress or Energy.

What's the difference between Meditate and Explore?

Meditate holds the sessions you'll come back to, with filters. Explore is for finding methods you haven't tried yet.

Do I need an account first?

No. You can try a session as a guest and create an account when you want to save your progress. After that you stay signed in. If you already have one, use Sign in at the top of this page.

Does Orbit track streaks and points?

Your Profile shows minutes, a streak and a few milestone badges. There are no points to spend, no leaderboards and no comparison with other people. The home screen doesn't show a streak.
